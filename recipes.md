## Masala Omelette (Indian)
Dietary: vegetarian, gluten-free, contains-egg, quick | Serves 1 | 10 min

Ingredients:
- 2 pcs eggs
- 0.25 cup onion
- 0.25 cup tomato
- 1 pcs green chilli
- 1 tbsp coriander
- 1 tbsp oil
- 0.25 tsp salt

Steps:
1. Beat the eggs with salt.
2. Mix in chopped onion, tomato, chilli and coriander.
3. Heat oil in a pan and pour the mixture.
4. Cook 2-3 minutes per side until set.

## Vegetable Fried Rice (Chinese)
Dietary: vegan, contains-soy, quick | Serves 2 | 20 min

Ingredients:
- 2 cup cooked rice
- 1 cup mixed vegetables
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp spring onion
- 2 tbsp oil

Steps:
1. Heat oil, saute garlic.
2. Add vegetables and stir-fry 3 minutes.
3. Add rice and soy sauce, toss on high heat.
4. Garnish with spring onion.

## Spaghetti Aglio e Olio (Italian)
Dietary: vegan, contains-gluten | Serves 2 | 20 min

Ingredients:
- 200 g spaghetti
- 4 cloves garlic
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 2 tbsp parsley
- 1 tsp salt

Steps:
1. Boil spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
2. Warm olive oil and gently cook sliced garlic and chilli flakes.
3. Toss drained pasta in the oil with a splash of pasta water.
4. Finish with parsley.

## Chicken Curry (Indian)
Dietary: contains-dairy, gluten-free | Serves 4 | 40 min

Ingredients:
- 500 g chicken
- 2 pcs onion
- 2 pcs tomato
- 0.5 cup yogurt
- 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 tbsp garam masala
- 3 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt

Steps:
1. Saute onions in oil until golden.
2. Add ginger garlic paste, then tomatoes and spices.
3. Add chicken and yogurt, cook till sealed.
4. Simmer covered 20 minutes until cooked through.

## Pancakes (American)
Dietary: vegetarian, contains-gluten, contains-egg, contains-dairy | Serves 3 | 20 min

Ingredients:
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup milk
- 1 pcs eggs
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder

Steps:

## Tomato Soup (Continental)

Ingredients:
- 6 pcs tomato
- 1 pcs onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp butter
- 0.5 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt

Steps:
1. Boil tomato, onion and garlic until soft.
